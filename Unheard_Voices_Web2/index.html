World Health Organization (WHO): In 2020, WHO estimated about 360 million people (including children) worldwide have disabling hearing loss. Without sign language, deaf babies are at risk of Language Deprivation Syndrome. This syndrome is characterized by a lack of access to naturally occurring language acquisition during their critical language-learning years. It can seriously impact their lives, such as relationships, education, and employment.
